About Me

I'm a Technical Lead at Aqua Security, setting technical direction for an engineering group of 10+ and owning critical paths of a cloud-native vulnerability and supply-chain security platform used by enterprise customers across AWS, Azure, and GCP.

My focus is on turning ambiguous production and customer problems into concrete designs and code — driving high-throughput scan workflows, service boundaries between scanners and the control plane, and platform scaling that took daily scan operations from roughly 10K to 10M.

Before Aqua, I worked across identity governance, enterprise HR systems, and access management at CA Technologies (Broadcom) and Oracle — giving me a grounding in security, distributed systems, and enterprise-scale software that carries through everything I build today.
